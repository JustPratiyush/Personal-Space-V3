# Background montage: sources

`background.mp4` is an original 54-second edit made for this project from public-domain archival footage. Every source was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-12, and its copyright status was checked against the license category on its Commons page.

The edit is black and white with lifted contrast, center-cropped to 1280×720 and slowed to 0.85×. It has 0.6 s dissolves and loops seamlessly. There's no audio.
