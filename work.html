Retrium OS

Browser OS · RPG · Vanilla JS

A retro operating system that runs in your browser and slowly turns into a role-playing game. You log in as Dash, poke around the Finder, the Terminal, the Messages and the Journal, and uncover what's hidden in the machine.

It started as my portfolio site. I designed the pixel-art icons, the UI and the storyline, and wrote it in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The Community guestbook runs on Upstash Redis.
