Hi, I'm Abhinav. I'm 21. By way of Mussoorie, Roorkee and Jaipur.

I build products, then make people care about them.

I'm in my final year of engineering at Manipal University Jaipur. I'm fascinated by neuroscience and how the brain works, I started building for the web at the end of 2024, and right now I'm learning to build LLMs from scratch.

I haven't picked a lane yet: design engineering, devrel, launches, content, AI engineering. So I build in public and see what sticks.
